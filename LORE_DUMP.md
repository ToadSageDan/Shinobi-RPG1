# Shinobi-RPG1 — World Lore Codex

## The Country: Quiet Steel Confederacy

The modern state is the **Quiet Steel Confederacy**, formerly the **Five Banner Marches**.  
It survived a century of treaty fraud, succession coups, and proxy assassinations that turned supply roads into warfronts.  
The current era is a fragile reconstruction: villages are rebuilding, but sabotage rings, forged command seals, shrine manipulation, and black-market war finance still threaten collapse.

Core tension: every faction claims to protect peace, but each controls a different pressure system—food, maps, oaths, debt, shrine rites, or courier routes.

---

## Arc Spine (Narrative Backbone)

1. **Political Warfront** — control of courier lanes and council leverage starts the conflict.
2. **Fracture Front** — attrition warfare and supply collapse test regional alliances.
3. **Recovery Mandate** — ports, medicine, and civilian rebuilding become strategic.
4. **Highland Reckoning** — ridge sovereignty and stormline signaling decide upper-border rule.
5. **Depths Awakening** — subterranean relic power and buried regency tech threaten surface stability.

These arcs now align directly with their key bosses and regions.

## Regions and Points of Interest (POI Function in Story)

- **Verdant Gate**: courier chokepoints and first sovereignty conflict.
- **Ashen Cradle**: wartime industry, fuel economy, and siege doctrine.
- **Tideglass Basin**: flood control, refugee ports, and medical supply legitimacy.
- **Stormwall Ridge**: stormline signaling, legal borders, and highland command.
- **Sunken Hollow**: buried regency infrastructure, toxin labs, and relic destabilization.

Each region’s POIs are structured as:
- **Hub** (civil function),
- **Conflict corridor/chokepoint** (contest function),
- **Specialized node** (ritual/industry/infrastructure function),
- **Boss arena** (arc climax function).
